Started at the municipal archive straight out of college and stayed longer than she meant to. Her father was a clerk for the city; she learned early that the official explanation and the explanation people used in the break room could be completely different things.

During the West Wing recovery, she was asked to reconcile a temporary inventory with the final list of damaged and transferred records. Several entries had changed status. One bound register was first listed as recovered and incomplete, then marked as destroyed in the version sent to the insurer. She couldn't find a signed transfer or disposal record to explain the change.

She refused to sign the revised list. That didn't stop the document from circulating with her initials on the scan, which is one reason she eventually left. She couldn't prove who had added them, and the longer she stayed, the more her objection was being described as a personality problem rather than a records problem.

She didn't leave immediately. She stayed through the rest of the recovery, trained the temporary staff, and tried to get a correction logged. By the time she resigned, nobody was openly threatening her; the pressure was mostly meetings that went nowhere, unanswered emails, and being left off decisions she was supposed to advise on.

Tomas thinks she walked away when the job got ugly. She thinks he has no idea how long she tried to fix it from inside.
