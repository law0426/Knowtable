Former municipal archivist, mid-thirties. Left the archive after refusing to sign a revised inventory following the West Wing fire. She is back in the building now because an old colleague has told her that a register she thought was destroyed is still there.

Good at noticing omissions, changed dates, inconsistent numbering and people answering a slightly different question from the one she asked. Not a detective and not especially good at reading people when they aren't talking about something concrete.

She likes records because they preserve the small, unglamorous steps that people forget they took. That belief is becoming a weakness: she trusts the record more than the person, even though she knows a record can be made to lie.

The scraps of paper in her desk are not sentimental keepsakes, at least not in the way Tomas thinks. Some are reminders of details she couldn't reconcile at the time. A few are probably just clutter now. Don't explain the habit too neatly.

Arc: begins wanting the archive's record corrected. Ends having to choose what to make public, and what damage she is willing to cause by doing so.
